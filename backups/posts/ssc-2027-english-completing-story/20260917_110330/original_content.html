৪. গল্প ও ডায়ালগে পূর্ণ নম্বর পাওয়ার ৪টি পেশাদার নিয়ম (Writing Strategies)

  • গল্পে উপযুক্ত শিরোনাম (Suitable Title): গল্পের শুরুতে একটি উপযুক্ত শিরোনাম লিখলে বোর্ড নিয়মে ২ নম্বর নিশ্চিত হয়। গল্পের মূলভাব স্পষ্ট করে এমন শিরোনাম দিন (যেমন: "Where There is a Will, There is a Way")।
  • গল্পের ধারাবাহিক সমাপ্তি (Logical Resolution): প্রশ্নের দেওয়া প্রম্পটের বাক্যগুলো প্রথমে তুলবেন এবং তারপর নিজের ভাষায় গল্পের প্লটকে পূর্ণাঙ্গ রূপ দেবেন। হঠাৎ করে গল্প শেষ না করে একটি চমৎকার শিক্ষণীয় নীতিকথা (Moral) দিয়ে শেষ করুন।
  • ডায়ালগের সম্ভাষণ ও বিদায় (Greeting & Farewell): ডায়ালগ শুরু করবেন স্বাভাবিক সালাম বা কুশল বিনিময়ের মাধ্যমে (যেমন: "Assalamu Alaikum" বা "Hello, how are you?") এবং শেষে আন্তরিক বিদায় সম্ভাষণ (যেমন: "Thank you for this fruitful discussion") দিয়ে শেষ করবেন।
  • ১০–১২টি তথ্যবহুল সংলাপ টার্ন (Conversational Turns): প্রতিটি চরিত্র কমপক্ষে ৫–৬ বার কথা বলবে (মোট ১০–১২টি টার্ন)। শুধু "Yes", "No" বা "I agree" না বলে যুক্তি ও তথ্যপূর্ণ পূর্ণাঙ্গ বাক্যে বক্তব্য উপস্থাপন করুন।

Story 01: The Wise Use of a Lucky Ticket

উৎস: NCTB Model Completing Story (Page 26)

"Motin is a poor farmer living in a village. His wife Dilara is an honest and pious woman. They have old parents and two school-going children living with them. So, they had to maintain the family with great difficulty. One day, Dilara bought a ticket and kept it in her bag. When the draw took place, she found that her ticket had won ten lakh taka."

Motin is a poor farmer living in a village. His wife Dilara is an honest and pious woman. They have old parents and two school-going children living with them. So, they had to maintain the family with great difficulty. One day, Dilara bought a ticket and kept it in her bag. When the draw took place, she found that her ticket had won ten lakh taka.

Dilara could not believe her eyes. She checked the number again and again. Then she called Motin. Motin was shocked to hear the news. Both of them thanked Allah for this great blessing.

At first, they thought about buying a big house and costly things. But Dilara said, "Money can finish quickly if we waste it." Motin agreed with her. They decided to use the money wisely.

First, they repaired their old house. Then they paid for Motin's father's treatment. They also bought books and clothes for their children. Motin bought a small piece of land to grow more crops. This helped him earn more money.

Dilara did not forget the poor people of the village. She gave some money to a widow. She also helped an orphan boy return to school. Everyone in the village praised her kind heart.

After some years, their life became better. Their children continued their studies. Motin's family lived in peace and happiness.

Motin often said, "The ticket gave us money, but Dilara's wisdom gave us a better life." Thus, their sudden good luck became a source of lasting happiness for many people around them.

Once upon a time, two friends went on a journey. They had to go through a forest. As they came through the woods, they saw a bag lying on the ground. Upon opening it, they discovered it was filled with gold coins.

Both of them were overjoyed at this unexpected treasure. However, greed soon crept into their hearts. Neither wanted to share the gold equally with the other.

As evening approached, they grew tired and hungry. One friend decided to go to the nearby village market to purchase some food, while the other remained behind to guard the gold bag.

While walking to the village, the friend thought, "If I poison the food, my companion will eat it and die. Then all the gold will be mine alone!" Accordingly, he bought deadly poison and mixed it thoroughly into the food.

Meanwhile, the friend guarding the bag thought, "Why should I split half of this fortune? As soon as he returns, I will strike him from behind and kill him."

When the friend returned with the poisoned food, the guard sprang upon him with a heavy stick and beat him to death on the spot. Pleased with his cunning deed, the murderer sat down and ate the delicious poisoned food hungrily.

Within moments, the deadly poison took effect. He fell to the ground writhing in agony and died beside his companion.

Thus, both greedy friends lost their lives, and the bag of gold remained untouched in the lonely forest. Indeed, as you sow, so shall you reap.

Story 03: Robert Bruce and the Spider (Failure is the Pillar of Success)

উৎস: Exclusive Model Test (Question 10)

"Robert Bruce was a famous king. Once his kingdom was invaded by his enemies. He fought courageously but lost the battle. In order to save his life from the opponent attack, he fled away from his kingdom and took shelter in a remote cave. The king was always in a pensive mood..."

Robert Bruce was a famous king. Once his kingdom was invaded by his enemies. He fought courageously but lost the battle. In order to save his life from the opponent attack, he fled away from his kingdom and took shelter in a remote cave. The king was always in a pensive mood for the loss of his throne and the misery of his subjects.

Six times he had mobilized his loyal soldiers and launched fierce battles against the invaders, but each time his army was beaten back with heavy casualties. He felt utterly despondent and thought of abandoning the struggle altogether.

While lying in the dark cave, a small movement caught his eye. He noticed a tiny spider trying to swing from one rocky ledge to another in order to weave its web. The distance was wide, and each time the spider swung forward, it slipped and fell down.

Bruce watched intently. The spider failed on its first attempt, second attempt, and third attempt. It tried four, five, and six times—failing each time just like Bruce had failed in his six battles.

"Surely the little creature will give up now," Bruce thought.

To his astonishment, the spider did not lose courage. Gathering all its strength, it made a seventh leap with supreme determination. This time, the silk thread caught the opposite rock, and the spider successfully completed its web.

A wave of fresh hope surged through Bruce's heart. He exclaimed, "If a tiny spider can succeed after six failures, why should I, the King of Scotland, surrender?"

Bruce immediately left the cave, rallied his loyal countrymen, and attacked the enemy forces with renewed vigor. Inspired by his fearless leadership, the Scottish army achieved a glorious victory and liberated their motherland.

Bruce learned that perseverance and unyielding courage can overcome the greatest adversities in life.

Dialogue 01: Dialogue between Sajid and His Father on Prevention of Dengue Fever

Sajid: Father, what is dengue fever, and why is everyone so worried about it?

Father: Dengue is a viral fever transmitted by the female Aedes mosquito. It causes high fever, severe headache, and vomiting.

Sajid: I also heard that people get a lot of pain in their joints.

Father: Yes, it is sometimes called "break-bone fever" because of that severe joint pain.

Sajid: How can we prevent these mosquitoes from growing?

Father: We must make sure there is no stagnant water around our house.

Sajid: Like in flower pots, discarded tyres, or empty tins?

Father: Exactly. They lay their eggs in clean, standing water.

Sajid: What should we do to protect ourselves at home?

Father: We should use mosquito nets while sleeping and wear long-sleeved clothes.

Sajid: Should we also use mosquito coils or sprays?

Father: Yes, but keeping our surroundings clean and dry is the best protection.

Sajid: I will check our balcony right now for any trapped water.

Father: Good job, Sajid. Public awareness is the first step to staying safe.
