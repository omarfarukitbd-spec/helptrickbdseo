১. সাহিত্য অংশ: নম্বর বণ্টন ও প্রশ্ন কাঠামো (Marks Distribution)

২. ৭টি নির্বাচিত কবিতা: মূলভাব, কাব্যিক অলংকার ও স্টার রেটিং (All 7 Poems for SSC 2027)

নিচে পাঠ্যক্রমের ৭টি কবিতার স্টার রেটিং, কবি, অন্তর্নিহিত ভাবার্থ এবং প্রধান আলংকারিক কৌশল (Poetic Devices) তুলনামূলক ছকে সাজানো হলো:

ক্র. স্টার কবিতা ও কবি মূল দর্শন ও থিম (Core Theme) কাব্যিক অলংকার (Poetic Devices)
1 *** Time, You Old Gipsy Man
কবি: Ralph Hodgson		 The relentless and irreversible passage of time and humanity's yearning to pause precious moments. Personification of Time as an old gipsy wanderer, imagery of bells, golden rings, and peacocks.
2 *** Stopping by Woods on a Snowy Evening
কবি: Robert Frost		 The delicate balance between admiring the serene beauty of nature and fulfilling moral responsibilities before death. Repetition ('And miles to go before I sleep'), visual and auditory imagery (dark woods, harness bells, easy wind).
3 *** O Me! O Life!
কবি: Walt Whitman		 The struggle with existential disillusionment, foolishness, and despair, answered by the triumphant joy that life exists and one may contribute a verse. Free verse, rhetorical questions, philosophical reflection, anaphora.
4 ** The Sands of Dee
কবি: Charles Kingsley		 Tragedy of rural life where Mary drowns in a raging western sea tide while bringing cattle home. Ballad rhythm, refrain ('Call the cattle home'), haunting natural imagery of rolling mist and wild foam.
5 ** To be Remembered
কবি: EFT Textbook Selection		 The unconditional bond of motherly love through all evolving stages of life and filial duty to care for aging parents. Emotional lyricism, nostalgic contrast between childhood and adulthood.
6 ** Sea-Fever
কবি: John Masefield		 The uncontrollable, passionate wanderlust of a sailor yearning to return to the lonely sea and the starry sky. Vivid maritime sensory imagery (grey dawn, wild call, flung spray, blown spume).
7 * Each Book is a Magic Box
কবি: EFT Textbook Selection		 The transformative magic of books as treasure chests that transport readers to distant realms and wisdom. Extended metaphor (book as magic box), childlike wonder, imaginative imagery.

৩. ২৩টি নির্বাচিত ছোটগল্প: প্লট, চরিত্র ও শিক্ষণীয় নীতিকথা (All 23 Stories for SSC 2027)

নিচে ২৩টি কালজয়ী ছোটগল্পের স্টারভিত্তিক গুরুত্ব, মূল চরিত্র এবং নৈতিক শিক্ষা তুলে ধরা হলো:

ক্র. স্টার গল্প ও রচয়িতা মূল প্লট ও দর্শন (Theme) মূল চরিত্রসমূহ (Key Characters)
1 *** The Merchant of Venice
লেখক: William Shakespeare		 Justice tempered with mercy, true friendship between Antonio and Bassanio, and defeat of greed and revenge. Antonio (generous merchant), Bassanio (devoted friend), Shylock (cruel moneylender), Portia (wise heroine).
2 *** The Story of an Hour
লেখক: Kate Chopin		 The complex psychology of female independence, sudden grief, and the tragic shock of lost freedom. Mrs. Louise Mallard, Brently Mallard, Josephine, Richards.
3 *** The Purple Jar
লেখক: Maria Edgeworth		 The consequence of impulsive vanity over practical utility; learning through painful experience. Rosamond (impulsive 7-year-old girl), Rosamond's mother (wise, disciplined guide).
4 *** The Gift of the Magi
লেখক: O. Henry		 Selfless, sacrificial love where mutual devotion transcends grinding poverty. Della (sacrifices hair for watch chain), Jim (sacrifices gold watch for combs).
5 *** The Last Leaf
লেখক: O. Henry		 Hope, supreme artistic sacrifice, and the enduring power of unconditional friendship. Johnsy (pneumonia patient), Sue (loyal friend), Behrman (old artist who paints masterpiece).
6 *** The Necklace
লেখক: Guy de Maupassant		 The tragic ruin caused by false pride, vanity, and the deception of social appearances. Mathilde Loisel (vain wife), Monsieur Loisel (devoted husband), Madame Forestier.
7 *** The Luncheon
লেখক: William Somerset Maugham		 Social hypocrisy, culinary extravagance, and humorous irony of financial vulnerability. The young writer (struggling narrator), The lady guest (gluttonous flatterer).
8 *** A Mother in Mannville
লেখক: Marjorie Kinnan Rawlings		 Integrity, pride of an orphan, longing for maternal affection, and emotional vulnerability. Jerry (orphan boy at Carolina mountain orphanage), The writer (narrator), Pat (dog).
9 ** The Old Man at the Bridge
লেখক: Ernest Hemingway		 The tragic impact of modern warfare on innocent, helpless civilians and animals. The old man (76-year-old peasant from San Carlos), The war scout (narrator).
10 * The Selfish Giant
লেখক: Oscar Wilde		 Redemption through love, compassion for innocent children, and spiritual rebirth. The Giant, The Little Boy (Christ child), The children.
11 * The Happy Prince
লেখক: Oscar Wilde		 Selfless sacrifice for the poor and suffering of society; spiritual beauty over physical grandeur. The Happy Prince (golden statue), The Little Swallow.
12 * The Open Window
লেখক: Saki (H.H. Munro)		 Deception through brilliant romance on short notice; psychological vulnerability. Framton Nuttel (nerve patient), Vera (15-year-old niece), Mrs. Sappleton.

৪. সাহিত্যভিত্তিক প্রশ্নে পূর্ণ ৮+৮ নম্বর অর্জনের ৪টি নিয়ম (Answering Strategy)

প্রশ্ন ৮ ও ৯-এ প্রতি প্রশ্নে ১ নম্বর নির্ধারিত থাকায় অতিরিক্ত বড় বা অপ্রাসঙ্গিক উত্তর লেখার কোনো প্রয়োজন নেই। নিচের ৪টি নিয়ম মানলে পূর্ণ ১৬ নম্বর পাওয়া সম্ভব:

  • সরাসরি ও প্রাসঙ্গিক উত্তর (Direct Answer): প্রশ্নের মূল উত্তরটি ১ম বাক্যেই দিয়ে দিন। যেমন: প্রশ্ন যদি হয় "Who is the poet of the poem?", উত্তর সরাসরি লিখবেন "Ralph Hodgson is the poet of the poem 'Time, You Old Gipsy Man'."
  • টেনস সঙ্গতি (Tense Consistency): প্রশ্নে Did থাকলে Past Tense এবং Does থাকলে Present Tense ব্যবহার করুন। সাহিত্যের ভাবার্থ বর্ণনায় সাধারণত Simple Present Tense ব্যবহার করা হয়।
  • ১ থেকে ২টি পূর্ণাঙ্গ বাক্য: প্রতিটি প্রশ্নের উত্তর ১ বা সর্বোচ্চ ২টি সুগঠিত বাক্যে শেষ করুন। এক শব্দে উত্তর না লিখে পূর্ণাঙ্গ বাক্যে উত্তর লেখা বোর্ড স্ট্যান্ডার্ড।
  • উদ্ধৃতি ও চরিত্র মূল্যায়ন: গল্পের ক্ষেত্রে চরিত্রের সঠিক নাম ও কবিতার ক্ষেত্রে অন্তর্নিহিত রূপক (Metaphor) এক বাক্যে ব্যাখ্যা করলে উত্তরটি মানসম্মত হয়।

৫. মডেল পরীক্ষা ০১: দাখিল ২০২৬ বোর্ড প্রশ্ন ও পূর্ণাঙ্গ সমাধান (১৬টি প্রশ্নোত্তর)

Question 8: Answer the following questions from Poems (1 × 8 = 8 Marks)

8(a): Do you like the poem 'To be Remembered'? Why?

উত্তর: Yes, I like the poem because it shows deep love between a mother and her daughter. It also teaches that we should love and care for our mothers, even when they grow old and change.

8(b): Why should children love both versions of their mother?

উত্তর: Children should love both versions of their mother because each phase of her life offers something important. As she changes, the love and care she gives may evolve, but it remains just as essential.

8(c): Why did the poet stop by the woods in 'Stopping by Woods on a Snowy Evening'?

উত্তর: The poet stopped by the woods to watch the snow falling quietly and admire the peaceful, dark, and deep forest scenery before continuing his journey.

8(d): How has the sea been described in the poem?

উত্তর: The sea has been described as a vast, powerful, and mysterious realm that devours life yet calls the sailor with an irresistible and wild call that cannot be denied.

8(e): What things were offered by the poet for the old gipsy man?

উত্তর: The poet offered: (i) Bells for Time's jennet, (ii) A great golden ring, (iii) Peacocks bowing to Time, (iv) Little boys singing for Time, and (v) Sweet girls festooning Time with may-flowers.

8(f): Write the name of the poet of the poem 'Time, You Old Gipsy Man'.

উত্তর: The poet of the poem 'Time, You Old Gipsy Man' is Ralph Hodgson.

8(g): What do you understand by the line 'Each Book is a Magic Box'?

উত্তর: The poet means that a child can unlock books with just a touch. Inside, books hold many wonders and surprises that magically transport readers to new places and give joy.

8(h): How did the milliner's shop look like in the textbook story?

উত্তর: The shop was decorated with beautiful ribbons and lace, with many colorful artificial flowers hanging in the windows, designed to catch the eyes of passers-by like Rosamond.

Question 9: Answer the following questions from Stories (1 × 8 = 8 Marks)

9(a): Who was Portia? What do you know about her?

উত্তর: Portia was a wealthy, beautiful, and exceptionally wise noblewoman from Belmont who married Bassanio after he chose the correct casket. She later disguised herself as a lawyer to save Antonio's life.

9(b): How did Portia and her friend disguise themselves in the courtroom?

উত্তর: Portia disguised herself as a young lawyer named Doctor Balthazar to defend Antonio, while her clever maid Nerissa accompanied her disguised as a lawyer's clerk.

9(c): Who was Shylock? What do you know about him?

উত্তর: Shylock was a greedy, cruel, and cunning moneylender of Venice. He hated Antonio for being kind and lending money without interest, and wanted revenge by cutting a pound of flesh from Antonio's body.

9(d): Who lent money to the poor without charging any interest?

উত্তর: Antonio was the generous merchant who lent money to the needy without charging interest, which is why the common people of Venice loved and respected him deeply.

9(e): How did Portia save Antonio's life?

উত্তর: Portia pointed out a loophole in the bond: Shylock was entitled to exact a pound of flesh, but if he shed a single drop of Christian blood, all his wealth would be forfeited to the state of Venice.

9(f): Who walked towards Antonio with a knife and why?

উত্তর: Shylock walked towards Antonio with a sharp knife to cut a pound of flesh from his breast as a contractual penalty because Antonio failed to repay the 3,000 ducats on time.

9(g): Who was the close friend of Antonio?

উত্তর: Bassanio was the intimate friend of Antonio. Antonio loved him so dearly that he risked his own life by signing the fatal bond to finance Bassanio's courtship in Belmont.

9(h): Sketch the character of Antonio.

উত্তর: Antonio was a wealthy, noble, and generous merchant of Venice who was selfless, honest, and ever-willing to sacrifice everything for his friends.

৬. মডেল পরীক্ষা ০২: এক্সক্লুসিভ মডেল টেস্ট ও পূর্ণাঙ্গ সমাধান (১৬টি প্রশ্নোত্তর)

Question 8: Answer the following questions from Poems (1 × 8 = 8 Marks)

8(a): What does the speaker ask Time to do in the poem 'Time, You Old Gipsy Man'?

উত্তর: In the poem 'Time, You Old Gipsy Man', the poet asks Time to stop or pause its relentless journey, even if just for a single day, offering him various precious gifts.

8(b): What is the main theme of the poem 'Time, You Old Gipsy Man'?

উত্তর: The main theme is the fleeting, irreversible nature of Time and the universal human desire to pause its rapid flight in order to savor the beautiful moments of life.

8(c): What is the main theme of the poem 'Stopping by Woods on a Snowy Evening'?

উত্তর: The main theme is the conflict between the desire to immerse oneself in the enchanting peace of nature and the necessity of keeping promises and fulfilling worldly duties before death.

8(d): Where does the speaker stop in 'Stopping by Woods on a Snowy Evening'?

উত্তর: The speaker stops between the frozen lake and the dark, snow-covered woods of a village neighbor to watch the gentle flakes fill up the trees.

8(e): Who is Mary in the poem of your textbook?

উত্তর: Mary is the innocent daughter of a poor peasant living beside the estuary of the River Dee who goes out alone across the marsh to bring the cattle home.

8(f): What was the weather like when Mary went out?

উত্তর: The weather was dangerous and stormy, characterized by wild, blinding western winds and thick rolling sea foam that concealed the treacherous rising tide.

8(g): What is the poem 'O Me! O Life!' about?

উত্তর: The poem 'O Me! O Life!' explores the existential meaning and purpose of human existence despite recurring struggles, human foolishness, and endless disappointments.

8(h): Who is the speaker in the poem 'O Me! O Life!'?

উত্তর: The speaker is a disillusioned and soul-searching version of the poet Walt Whitman himself, questioning the worth of life and concluding that merely existing and contributing a verse makes life sublime.

Question 9: Answer the following questions from Stories (1 × 8 = 8 Marks)

9(a): Who had brought the news of Mr. Brently Mallard's death in 'The Story of an Hour'?

উত্তর: Mr. Mallard's close friend Richards brought the news. He was in the newspaper office when the list of rail disaster fatalities was received and saw Brently Mallard's name at the top.

9(b): How did Mr. Mallard die, according to the rumour?

উত্তর: According to the telegraph report, Mr. Mallard was killed in a catastrophic railroad disaster.

9(c): Who was already in the court of justice when Bassanio reached Venice?

উত্তর: Antonio, along with Shylock and the Duke of Venice, was already in the courtroom awaiting the trial to begin.

9(d): What were they waiting for in the court of justice?

উত্তর: They were waiting for the arrival of Doctor Bellario, a learned jurist of Padua who had been summoned by the Duke to judge the legal dispute.

9(e): When did the Duke enter the courtroom?

উত্তর: The Duke entered the courtroom at the commencement of the session and took his seat on the judicial bench before summoning Antonio and Shylock.

9(f): Who is Rosamond in 'The Purple Jar'?

উত্তর: Rosamond is a seven-year-old impulsive, inquisitive girl who is easily fascinated by pretty, glittering but useless ornamental objects.

9(g): Where were Rosamond and her mother walking?

উত্তর: Rosamond and her mother were walking down the busy streets of London, window-shopping and looking at various retail displays.

9(h): What did Rosamond want to do when she saw the shop windows?

উত্তর: When Rosamond saw the chemist's shop window, she was enchanted by the sparkling purple jar and desperately wanted to buy it instead of necessary new shoes.

৭. সচরাচর জিজ্ঞাসা (FAQ)

Question 1: প্রশ্ন ৮ ও ৯-এর উত্তরে কত বড় বাক্য লেখা উচিত?

উত্তর: যেহেতু প্রতিটি প্রশ্নে ১ নম্বর বরাদ্দ, তাই ১ থেকে সর্বোচ্চ ২টি পূর্ণাঙ্গ ব্যাকরণিক বাক্যে সুনির্দিষ্ট উত্তর লেখাই আদর্শ। অপ্রয়োজনীয় বড় প্যারাগ্রাফ লেখার প্রয়োজন নেই।

Question 2: কবি ও লেখকের নামের বানান ভুল হলে কি নম্বর কাটা যায়?

উত্তর: হ্যাঁ, বিখ্যাত সাহিত্যিকদের নামের বানান (যেমন: Ralph Hodgson, Robert Frost, Walt Whitman, Guy de Maupassant) অবশ্যই শতভাগ নির্ভুলভাবে লিখতে হবে। ভুল বানানে নম্বর কাটা হতে পারে।

Question 3: কবিতায় Poetic Devices থেকে কি প্রশ্ন হতে পারে?

উত্তর: হ্যাঁ! বিশেষ করে Personification, Metaphor, Alliteration এবং Imagery নিয়ে প্রায়ই প্রশ্ন হয় (যেমন: "How is Time portrayed?"). তাই কবিতার রূপক অর্থ বুঝে রাখা জরুরি।
