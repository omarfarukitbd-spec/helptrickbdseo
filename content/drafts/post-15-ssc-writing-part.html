মডেল ০১: A School Library / A Madrasah Library ১০ নম্বর

A school library is an indispensable part of an educational institution. It is regarded as a storehouse of knowledge where students and teachers satisfy their intellectual thirst. Our school has a spacious and well-decorated library situated on the second floor of the main academic building. It houses more than five thousand books arranged neatly in large steel almirahs according to various subjects like literature, history, science, geography, biography, and religion. Besides textbooks, there are leading national dailies, periodicals, and encyclopedias. The library has a calm, tranquil environment that encourages concentrated reading. There is a large reading room with long tables and comfortable chairs where around fifty students can read together silently. Strict discipline is maintained inside the library; talking or making noise is strictly prohibited. Two qualified librarians manage the library efficiently with a modern catalog system. A student possessing a library card can borrow two books at a time for two weeks. A library broadens our mental horizon and instills a love for reading beyond the curriculum. In fact, an ideal school library is the true intellectual lighthouse of any campus.

মডেল ০২: Tree Plantation and Environmental Preservation ১০ নম্বর

Tree plantation means planting and nurturing trees in a planned way for the benefit of mankind and nature. Trees are our greatest and most faithful companions. They supply us with oxygen which is indispensable for our respiration, and they absorb harmful carbon dioxide from the atmosphere. In addition, trees provide us with sweet fruits, timber, medicines, shade, and aesthetic beauty. They preserve the fertility of topsoil, prevent soil erosion, and protect coastal regions from fierce tidal surges and cyclones. But it is a matter of profound concern that people are cutting down trees recklessly for quick timber trade and urban expansion. As an alarming consequence, global temperatures are rising, polar ice caps are melting, and natural disasters are ravaging our lands. If this trend of deforestation goes unchecked, our motherland will soon turn into an arid desert. To combat climate catastrophe, extensive tree plantation drives should be launched across the country during the rainy season. Open lands beside highways, railway tracks, embankments, and school compounds must be brought under afforestation. We should plant more trees today to ensure a greener, healthier planet for future generations.

মডেল ০৩: Smart Bangladesh: Vision of Tomorrow ১০ নম্বর

Smart Bangladesh is an ambitious national roadmap that aims to transform Bangladesh into an innovative, technologically advanced, and knowledge-based prosperous economy. Following the monumental success of Digital Bangladesh, the vision of Smart Bangladesh rests on four core foundational pillars: Smart Citizen, Smart Economy, Smart Government, and Smart Society. In a Smart Bangladesh, every citizen will possess digital literacy and computational competence. Governance will be completely paperless, transparent, and seamlessly accessible to citizens through automated digital portals. The economy will shift towards artificial intelligence, robotics, blockchain technology, and cashless financial transactions. Rural health complexes, agricultural markets, and classrooms will be interconnected through high-speed broadband internet. This visionary leap will eliminate poverty, minimize the urban-rural divide, and create massive self-employment opportunities for the energetic youth. However, to translate this dream into reality, cybersecurity infrastructure must be fortified, and uninterrupted digital education must reach every remote village. With collective enthusiasm and patriotic determination, Smart Bangladesh will establish itself as a prestigious leader on the global stage.

৩. শীর্ষ শর্ট কম্পোজিশন মডেল (Short Compositions — 20 Marks)

মডেল ০১: My Aim in Life (আমার জীবনের লক্ষ্য) ২০ নম্বর

1. Introduction:

A life without an aim is like a rudderless ship drifting aimlessly in a stormy sea. An aim fixes a person's goal and channels his energies, determination, and labor toward a fruitful destination. Different people cherish different ambitions according to their tastes and outlooks. Some hanker after material wealth, while others seek political power. My aim in life is neither wealth nor fame; my cherished aim is to become an honest, dedicated medical doctor to serve the poor rural people of Bangladesh.

2. Reasons for My Choice:

Bangladesh is predominantly an agricultural country where the vast majority of our population resides in remote villages. Unfortunately, healthcare facilities in our rural regions are deplorably inadequate. Poor village folks frequently fall prey to preventable epidemics and chronic ailments due to the scarcity of qualified physicians. Greedy quacks exploit their ignorance and extort hard-earned money. Witnessing their heart-rending suffering during my childhood left an indelible impression on my mind and inspired me to dedicate my life to humanitarian medicine.

3. Preparation for My Goal:

I know that securing admission into a reputed government medical college requires extraordinary academic brilliance and unwavering devotion. Therefore, I am focusing intensely on my science subjects, particularly Biology, Chemistry, and Physics. I spend my study hours with disciplined regularity and seek guidance from my revered teachers.

4. Future Plan of Action:

After completing my MBBS degree, I will not stay in luxurious metropolitan cities or migrate abroad. I will return to my native village and establish a well-equipped charitable clinic. I will provide free medical treatment and medicines to distressed patients who cannot afford basic medical care. I also plan to organize weekly health hygiene campaigns to educate villagers on sanitary living.

5. Conclusion:

To serve suffering humanity is to serve the Almighty Creator. I believe that true happiness does not lie in accumulating wealth, but in bringing a smile to the distressed faces of suffering fellow countrymen. I pray to Almighty Allah that I may stay steadfast in my goal and serve my motherland selflessly.

মডেল ০২: The Wonders of Modern Science and Computer ২০ নম্বর

1. Introduction:

We live in an era of boundless scientific marvels. Science has transformed human civilization beyond recognition, turning fairy-tale imaginations into tangible realities. Among all the astonishing inventions of modern science, the computer stands out as the most supreme and miraculous crowning glory. It has become the nerve center of modern industry, education, communication, and governance.

2. Computer in Daily Life and Communication:

Today, computers coupled with the internet have reduced the vast globe into a tiny global village. Through electronic mail, video conferences, and social media platforms, communication across continents takes place in a fraction of a second. E-commerce and automated banking have eliminated physical queues, enabling seamless commercial transactions from home.

3. In the Realm of Education and Research:

In modern education, the computer plays the role of a virtual tutor and an infinite digital library. Students can attend online lectures from renowned world universities, access research journals, and solve complex scientific equations within milliseconds. Complex meteorological calculations and space explorations are completely unthinkable without supercomputers.

4. Demerits and Abuses of Science:

Science is a great blessing, but its misuse can be devastating. Excessive addiction to computer gaming and social platforms wastes valuable study hours of young students and impairs eyesight. Furthermore, destructive nuclear weapons of mass destruction pose an existential hazard to planetary survival.

5. Conclusion:

Science is a faithful servant but a tyrannical master. It depends entirely on human wisdom whether we use computer technology for human prosperity or self-annihilation. Used judiciously, modern science will lead human civilization toward an era of unprecedented enlightenment.
