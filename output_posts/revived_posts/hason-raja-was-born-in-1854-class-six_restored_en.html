1. Original English Reading Passage (Textbook)

Read the following seen passage carefully from your textbook and answer the questions that follow:

"Hason Raja was born in 1854 in a wealthy landlord family in Sylhet. He did not receive much formal education. In his youth, he led a life of luxury. He had everything — money, fame, and comfort. At one time he understood that all these are meaningless. He gave away all his properties and started to think deeply about the Creator, life, death, and mankind."

"Hason Raja wrote a lot of songs, perhaps about a thousand in number. His book called 'Hason Udash' was published in 1906. The book had 206 songs in it. In his songs, Hason called himself 'Crazy Hason Raja' or the 'Pagla Hason Raja'. A volume called 'Hason Raja Samagra' was also published. The book contained 500 poems and songs. Some of the songs were written by the poet in Hindi."

"Hason Raja's songs were always popular in Sylhet. But very soon they spread all over Bangladesh and beyond. In India's West Bengal, some folk singers made Hason's songs popular. When you grow older, you will learn more about this great mystic poet through reading and listening to his songs."

5. Model Question 2: Short Answer Questions with Solutions

Answer the following questions based on your reading of the passage:

Q1: Where and when was Hason Raja born? What kind of family did he come from?

Ans: Hason Raja was born in 1854 in Sylhet. He was born into an affluent and respected landlord (zamindar) family.

Q2: What change took place in Hason Raja's life, and why did he give away his wealth?

Ans: Although Hason Raja enjoyed a luxurious life in his youth, he eventually realized that worldly riches, comfort, and fame were fleeting and meaningless. Consequently, he gave away all his worldly possessions to devote his mind deeply to the Creator, spiritual truth, and humanity.

Q3: What are the two famous books of Hason Raja mentioned in the text?

Ans: The two books mentioned are 'Hason Udash' (published in 1906 with 206 songs) and 'Hason Raja Samagra' (a grand compilation containing 500 poems and songs).

Q4: How did Hason Raja's songs become popular outside Bangladesh?

Ans: While Hason Raja's songs initially captivated Sylhet and all of Bangladesh, talented folk singers in India's West Bengal began performing them, which helped his spiritual compositions gain widespread popularity beyond national borders.

7. Summary of the Passage in Simple English

Exam Model Summary (Within 5-6 Sentences):
Dewan Hason Raja was a prominent mystic poet who was born in 1854 into a wealthy landlord family in Sylhet. Despite enjoying an affluent and luxurious youth without receiving much formal schooling, he later underwent a spiritual metamorphosis and realized the vanity of material possessions. Giving away all his properties, he dedicated his existence to meditating on the Creator, life, and mankind. He authored around one thousand devotional songs, calling himself 'Pagla Hason Raja'. His major works, including 'Hason Udash' and 'Hason Raja Samagra', spread widely across Bangladesh and West Bengal, establishing him as one of the greatest mystic bards in Bengali literature.

8. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Why is Hason Raja called a mystic poet?
Ans: A mystic poet seeks a direct personal communion with the Creator through contemplation and love. Hason Raja looked beyond earthly possessions and reflected deeply on life, death, and God through his symbolic songs.
Q: Did Hason Raja write any songs in Hindi?
Ans: Yes, according to the textbook text, some of his songs were composed in Hindi alongside his mainstream Bengali compositions.
Q: Why did Hason Raja refer to himself as 'Pagla Hason Raja'?
Ans: In Sufi and Baul philosophy, 'Pagla' or crazy implies someone deeply intoxicated with the love of the Almighty, who abandons all worldly pride and ego.
